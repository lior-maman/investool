## What it does

Most scanners are a firehose. Investool is a filter. Pick a sector, turn one dial, and see only what actually moved — with a clear read on whether it's worth acting on.

| Signal | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Volume Scanner** | Relative volume, ranked hottest-first across the sector |
| **Breakout 30D** | Stocks closing above their 30-day high — and by how much |
| **Fear & Trend** | VIX, dollar strength, and the S&P trend, each scored 1–10 |
| **Breakout Quality** | Volatility compression + RSI + MACD + volume, in one 1–10 read |
| **Volatility** | Today's ATR ranked against its own trailing year |
| **Auto Analysis** | Volume + breakout read together into one call |

---

## Two scores, one verdict

A great setup in a fearful market is a different story from the same setup when the tape is calm. So Investool scores them **separately**:

- 🟢 **Stock Score (0–100)** — how strong is *this* setup? (breakout quality + volatility)
- 🟡 **Market Score (0–100)** — is it even the day to enter? (fear + dollar + S&P trend)

Then it calls it:

> **`BUY`** · **`WATCH`** · **`SKIP`**

A strong stock in a weak market never gets a BUY — the market gate makes sure of it.
